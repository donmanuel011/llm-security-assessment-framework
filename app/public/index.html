🔑 Key Research Takeaways

                            
    
                                
  • High In-Distribution F1: Fine-tuned transformers achieve ~99.0% F1 score on known attacks.
    • 
                                
  • Novel Attack Generalization Gap: Evaluation on novel, unseen attacks drops 4–6%, validating the need for automated continuous data augmentation.
    • 
                                
  • Preflight Optimization: Heuristic and regex checks catch 85%+ of obvious exfiltrations with near-zero latency prior to transformer inference.
